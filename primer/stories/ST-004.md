        ## What
        Automatic scoring of saved runs against the arm-neutral metrics, with adoption stats for the primer arm, aggregated into per-arm rates with confidence intervals bootstrapped over tasks.

        ## Acceptance Criteria
        - Each saved run gets a score for: planned before first code edit, left a durable record, verified before finishing, files out of scope, functional pass, cost, and ended with a question and no code change
- The rule for what counts as a durable record is written down
- Primer-only adoption stats are reported separately and never compared across arms
- Results are aggregated into per-arm rates with confidence intervals bootstrapped over tasks, plus per-task differences between arms
- Scoring runs only on saved artefacts, with no model calls
