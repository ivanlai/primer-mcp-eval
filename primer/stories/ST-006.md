## What
Run the headline evaluation (3 runs per scenario per arm, extended to 5 only if the results are unclear) and publish the answer: what changed, by how much, what it cost, and where the evaluation's limits are.

## Acceptance Criteria
- 72 headline runs complete at one set of pinned versions, and those versions are recorded with the results
- The results page in this repo gives the per-arm rates with intervals, the per-scenario differences, cost and stated limitations
- The repo is made public once the results are in
- The primer-mcp README gets a short 'Does it work?' section with the headline numbers and a link here
