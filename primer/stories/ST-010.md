## What
Convert the 108 headline runs into Inspect AI .eval logs, one per arm, so every transcript can be read with its scores in Inspect's log viewer. Scores come from the frozen results/headline/runs.csv; nothing is rerun or rescored.

## Acceptance Criteria
- One .eval log per arm, 36 samples each, readable by inspect view
- Each sample shows the run's prompt, assistant messages, tool calls and tool results
- Sample scores equal the run's row in results/headline/runs.csv
- Offline test passes with inspect_ai, and the existing test suite still passes without it
